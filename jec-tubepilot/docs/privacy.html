TubePilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · applies to the TubePilot browser extension, version 5.1 and later.

Summary

TubePilot has no server. Everything it stores stays in your browser (chrome.storage.local). It does not sell, share or transfer your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending. The publisher of TubePilot cannot see your videos, keys, channel profiles or analyses.

Your use of these Google services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, the Google Terms of Service, the YouTube Terms of Service. TubePilot uses YouTube API Services.

Page access

TubePilot runs only on studio.youtube.com, www.youtube.com and gemini.google.com. On YouTube Studio it reads the fields of the video you are editing (title, description, tags, file you choose) and writes the proposals you accept. On gemini.google.com it types the request, attaches the audio and reads the answer, only when you start an analysis. It does not read other websites, your browsing history, e-mails or passwords.

Retention and deletion

  • Analyses and cached searches stay in your browser until you delete them (History tab, Settings › Backup › Clear the cache) or uninstall the extension. Cached YouTube API data is refreshed or removed automatically (at most 30 days).
  • The Gemini conversation stays in your Gemini history, which you control at gemini.google.com (Activity).
  • You can revoke TubePilot’s access to your Google data at any time by removing your keys, and review Google’s security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

TubePilot is a tool for content creators and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the publisher through the support link of the store listing where you obtained TubePilot.
